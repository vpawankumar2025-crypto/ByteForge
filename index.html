Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What we collect
When you sign up: your name, email, and password (stored encrypted, never in plain text). When you enroll: payment confirmation via Razorpay (we don't store your card details — Razorpay handles this). When you generate an offer letter or certificate: your name, email, contact number, college name, register number, program dates, a typed digital signature, and a payment confirmation screenshot you upload.


2. How we use it
To create and manage your account, process enrollments, generate and email your offer letter/certificate, and let colleges or employers verify a certificate's authenticity via our public Verify Certificate page (which shows only your name, college, register number, program, and dates — never your email, phone, or password).


3. Where it's stored
Your data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database. Emails are sent via Gmail SMTP. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.


4. Your rights
You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by emailing bytefoge.courses@gmail.com. Note: certificates already issued and verifiable publicly may need to remain queryable for verification integrity even after an account deletion request — we'll discuss specifics with you if this applies.


5. Cookies & local storage
We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.


6. Security
Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text). All traffic to this site is encrypted (HTTPS). While we take reasonable precautions, no online system is 100% immune to security incidents.


7. Children's privacy
Our services are intended for students of college/pre-professional age and above. If you believe a minor has provided us personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we'll address it.


8. Contact
Privacy questions: bytefoge.courses@gmail.com

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

1. General policy
Once you've enrolled and paid for a course or internship, that enrollment is non-refundable, since content access unlocks immediately upon successful payment.


2. When you ARE eligible for a refund
If we fail to deliver what was promised — for example, your payment was successful but enrollment did not activate, or the course content is inaccessible due to an error on our end that we cannot resolve within a reasonable time — you are entitled to a full refund.


3. How to request a refund
Email bytefoge.courses@gmail.com with your registered email address, the course/internship name, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within 2 business days.


4. Processing time
Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank/card issuer.


5. Duplicate payments
If you're accidentally charged twice for the same enrollment, contact us with both payment references and we'll refund the duplicate charge in full.
